FAQ

Pricing questions

How much does a roof report cost?

A Premium Residential report is $12, Rapid Squares is $8, a Commercial report is $30 and a Storm Report is $5. With a subscription they are $7, $5, $25 and free.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every company can try a subscription free for 30 days, with subscriber prices on every report from the first day. A card is needed to start, and you can cancel any time before the trial ends.

How much is the subscription?

$100 a month, or $1,000 a year, which saves $200. It covers your whole team: everyone on the account orders at subscriber prices.

Do new accounts get free reports?

Yes. Create an account and your first Premium Residential reports are free.

Is the Xactimate ESX file extra?

Yes, $5 per report, on Premium Residential and Commercial reports. Add it when you order or later from the report page.

What if a roof can't be measured?

You're refunded automatically, and we email you to say why.

Are there contracts or minimums?

No. Pay per report with no account at all, or subscribe month to month or by the year and cancel any time.
