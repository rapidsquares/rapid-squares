Commercial reports are usually ready in about 8 hours. Large or complex properties can take longer.

Yes. Commercial reports are built for flat and low-slope roofs, including parapet walls, and list flat sections separately in the pitch breakdown.

Which buildings are measured?

Every building on the property.
