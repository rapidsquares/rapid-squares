FAQ

Rapid Squares questions

What is Rapid Squares?

A fast roof measurement: every building on the property outlined on an aerial photo, with its squares at an estimated pitch and at every pitch from 1/12 to 12/12, including 10%, 12% and 15% waste.

How fast is a Rapid Squares report?

Under 30 minutes from the order.

How is Rapid Squares different from a Premium Residential report?

Rapid Squares gives squares, pitch, footprint and perimeter for each building on one page. A Premium Residential report adds every edge length, length, area and pitch diagrams, and an optional Xactimate ESX file.

Does Rapid Squares include an Xactimate (ESX) file?

No. The ESX file comes with Premium Residential and Commercial reports.

How much does Rapid Squares cost?

Rapid Squares is $8, or $5 with a subscription.

What if I know the real pitch?

Use that row of the table: every report lists the squares at every pitch from 1/12 to 12/12, not only the estimated one.
